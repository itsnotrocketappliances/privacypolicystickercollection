Stickers4Ever — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026. Stickers4Ever is an iPhone app by an individual developer. It is a personal sticker book: photograph a sticker, cut it out, and keep it in a virtual album instead of sticking it down for good.

What Stickers4Ever does

The app stores the albums and stickers you create, lets you move stickers between albums, and — if you sign into iCloud on your device — syncs them to your other Apple devices through your own iCloud account. There is no account in the app itself: no name, email, or sign-up is ever asked for. Details are only needed if you choose to buy the one-time Pro upgrade, and that purchase is handled entirely by Apple.

Data we collect

We collect nothing. Stickers4Ever has no analytics, no tracking, no ads, and no server of its own. It does not collect, store on any server, sell, or share any personal data. We never see your stickers, your photos, or your albums.

Data stored on your device and in your iCloud

  • Your albums and stickers — the albums you create, the stickers you add (including the photos you choose and the cut-out images the app makes from them), and details you type such as a sticker's name, artist / company, and tags. These are stored on your iPhone, and — only if iCloud sync is on for the app — in your own iCloud account, in Apple's private CloudKit database for this app. That storage counts against your iCloud storage, not the developer's, and nobody else can see it.
  • Your Pro purchase state — whether this Apple ID owns the one-time Pro upgrade, so the app can keep it unlocked. This is checked with Apple through StoreKit; no purchase information is sent to the developer.

Data sent to third parties

The app contacts no third-party service at all. The only outside systems involved are Apple's own: iCloud (only when you have iCloud sync on, to sync your albums between your devices) and the App Store (only when you buy or restore the Pro upgrade). Your photos are never uploaded anywhere by the app — the camera and photo library are used only on your device, when you choose a photo to add.

Permissions — why each one is needed

  • Camera — only when you tap to photograph a sticker, so you can add it to a book.
  • Photo library — only when you choose a photo from your library, so you can add a sticker photo you already have.

Children

Stickers4Ever is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "last updated" date.

Contact
